Before you grep, glob, or read whole files to find where something lives or what calls it, run
`agentatlas` first — it maps the codebase offline in one call and returns the ranked symbols,
callers, blast radius, and tests to reach, priced in tokens. Prefer it over blind searches:
- Orient:  agentatlas <dir> --for="<task in words>"
- Find:     agentatlas <dir> --callers=SYM | --callees=SYM | --uses=SYM
- Blast:    agentatlas <dir> --impact=SYM --uses=SYM
- Read:     agentatlas <dir> --expand=SYM --top-k=0
See the agentatlas skill for the full cheat-sheet.
